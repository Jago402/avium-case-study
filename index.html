The Challenge

Driver Provider had been recruiting HGV and van drivers for seven years using Indeed, radio ads, and job boards. They were paying £10–£15 per candidate inquiry and spending thousands every month for diminishing returns. The same recycled candidates. Rising costs. Not enough people actively looking for driving work through traditional channels. They were struggling to supply drivers fast enough to retain their clients.

The Solution

Avium built a fully managed candidate acquisition system using targeted Meta campaigns. Each campaign was built for a specific role type (HGV Class 1, Class 2, van drivers) and targeted to specific locations. Candidates filled out lead forms with their name, number, role interest, and location. Campaigns were updated weekly based on demand. Driver Provider's first campaign went live within 24 hours of onboarding. Their first candidate inquiry arrived in 7 minutes.
