What happens next

Six steps from today to candidates landing in your inbox.

01

Payment

Once payment is in, we get started straight away.

02

Contract

We send a short contract setting out the scope of work. It protects you and us. No lock-in: it's rolling, and you can leave with 7 days' notice.

03

Onboarding call

We get you set up on the ad account together, and take a list of every role and location you need filling, plus your logos and branding.

04

We build, you approve

We create the ads and build a smart lead form around your criteria for each role. You approve everything before anything goes live.

05

Live

Once you've approved, roles are usually live within a couple of hours, and leads usually start coming in within an hour or two of going live.

06

Optimise and report

Every week we put more budget behind what's working and send you a report on your results.

What we need from you

The sooner we have these, the sooner you're live. Bring what you can to the onboarding call.

Logos and branding

  • Your logo, ideally a PNG with a transparent background
  • Any brand colours or guidelines you have

Meta access

  • Access to your Facebook page and ad account (steps below)
  • A card on your ad account. Ad spend is paid by you, straight to Meta

Every role you need filling, in this format

Rolee.g. HGV Class 1 Driver
Locatione.g. Dartford
SpecificationQualifications, experience and anything else needed, e.g. CPC, digi tacho card, 2 years' experience

Meta access

We run the ads from your own Facebook page and ad account, so everything stays in your name and under your control. We'll walk you through this on the onboarding call.

1. Business account

You'll need a Meta Business account (business.facebook.com). Don't have one? We'll set it up with you on the call.

2. Add us as a partner

In Business Settings, add Avium as a partner using the Business ID we send you, and give us access to your Facebook page and ad account.

3. Leads access

Give us access to your leads in Leads Access, so we can connect the lead forms and send candidates straight to you.

Your leads

This is where results are won or lost.

  • Leads land instantly in your inbox or CRM, the moment a candidate applies.
  • Call them fast. Candidates are most interested the minute they apply. The quicker you call, the more you place.
  • Every candidate is pre-screened on your criteria for the role before they reach you. Your team still checks documents as normal.
  • Tell us about bad leads. If something's off, let us know and we'll tighten the form or targeting.

FAQ

Do I need to do anything to the ads?

No. We build, run and optimise everything. You approve the ads and forms before they go live, and your team calls the leads.

Who pays for the ads?

You do, directly to Meta, on the card on your own ad account. Our fee is separate.

Whose name do the ads run under?

Yours. They run from your Facebook page and in your branding.

How quickly will I see candidates?

Usually within an hour or two of a role going live.

Can I pause or leave?

Yes. It's a rolling agreement with no lock-in, and you can leave with 7 days' notice.

What if I'm getting more candidates than my team can call?

Tell us and we'll lower the budget or pause a role. You're always in control of the volume.
